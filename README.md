## 🇨🇳 中文

把一句话故事创意做成角色和场景连贯的风格化 3D 动画短片，覆盖镜头规划、视频生成、配乐合成与质量复查。所有固定决策、批准、修订、模型与分辨率选择、继续/重做关口都走选项卡，不靠聊天里一句「继续」。流程固定为先故事后资产：角色卡与场景卡锁定后，先生成七列镜头表并做七项硬卡自检，再出单一文本分镜文档，最后逐镜生成视频、全片拼接与 BGM 合成、最终复查。针对多角色对白最高频的串戏问题，在入口层、镜头表层、Prompt 层、终检层四层堵死口型错乱。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 一句话创意 → 七列镜头表：每秒指令＋音频线索＋最终对白＋音频模式＋嘴部状态＋道具状态＋位置交接＋声音锁＋桥接节拍
- 音频模式是一级选择（静音／对白主导），整条流水线按该模式改写 prompt
- 每镜强制绑定说话人、非说话人闭嘴；终检把说话人身份与嘴部状态作为合格硬卡
- 单镜视频生成 → 全片拼接与 BGM 合成 → 最终复查，成片不残留分镜痕迹
- 质检只做判断、不自动返工：同一镜头同一问题最多初次生成＋两次追加尝试（共 3 次）
- 追加生成逐次授权；故事、资产、首轮生成的批准不自动覆盖返工

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a one-line story idea into a stylized 3D animated short with consistent characters and scenes, covering shot planning, video generation, music assembly and quality review. Every fixed decision, approval, revision, model/resolution choice and continue-or-redo gate runs through choice cards rather than a plain chat reply. The order is fixed: story before assets. Once character and scene cards are locked it builds a seven-column shot table with a seven-item self-check, then a single text storyboard document, then per-shot video, full assembly with BGM, and a final review. To fight the most common multi-character failure, lip-sync mix-ups are blocked at four layers: entry, shot table, prompt and final check.

### ✨ What it does

- One-line idea → seven-column shot table: per-second direction, audio cues, final dialogue, audio mode, mouth state, prop state, position handoff, sound lock and bridging beat
- Audio mode is a first-class choice (silent / dialogue-led) and rewrites every prompt in the pipeline
- Each shot binds the speaker and mutes non-speakers; the final check hard-gates speaker identity and mouth state
- Per-shot video → full assembly with BGM → final review, with no storyboard traces left in the cut
- Quality review judges only, never auto-reworks: one first generation plus at most two extra attempts per shot and issue (3 total)
- Every extra generation needs its own authorisation; approving story, assets or the first round does not cover rework
